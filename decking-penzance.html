Local know-how

Building decks for Penzance’s conditions

What we plan for when designing decking in and around Penzance.

  • Winter gales across Mount’s Bay

    Exposed decks need well-anchored sub-frames, stainless fixings and balustrades designed for wind loads.

  • Mild, damp climate

    Mild temperatures and moisture encourage algae, so grippy boards and good drainage are important.

  • Granite and rocky ground

    We plan posts and footings around rock and uneven ground to create solid, level decks.

What is the best decking for a windy, salty site in Penzance?

Capped composite boards on a solid sub-frame with marine-grade stainless fixings are a robust, low-maintenance choice for exposed sites near the bay.

Do you cover Marazion, Newlyn and Mousehole?

Yes, we cover Penzance, Newlyn, Marazion, Mousehole and the surrounding area of West Penwith.

Can you build a deck on rocky ground?

Yes. We plan footings and posts around rock and use adjustable supports where needed to build level decks.
