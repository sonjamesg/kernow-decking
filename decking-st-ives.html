Building decks for St Ives’s conditions

What we plan for when designing decking in and around St Ives.

  • Tight access and small plots

    Narrow lanes and limited parking mean careful planning, so materials and installation are organised around your property.

  • Conservation areas

    Parts of St Ives are within conservation areas, and some properties are listed. We help you check what permission may be needed.

  • Salt air and sea views

    Glass or wire balustrades keep the view open, and salt-tolerant materials and fixings keep everything looking good.

Can you work on properties with difficult access in St Ives?

Yes. We plan deliveries and installation around narrow lanes and limited parking, and can carry materials in by hand where needed.

Do I need permission for decking in a St Ives conservation area?

You may. Listed buildings and some conservation-area properties need extra consents. We will advise and help you check with Cornwall Council.

What decking is best for a St Ives holiday let?

Low-maintenance capped composite decking with a grippy finish is usually best, keeping upkeep low between guests.
