About us

A decking company Cornwall can count on

A decking company Cornwall homeowners can call, led by Neil and based near Newquay.

Meet Neil, our founder and installer

Neil has years of experience in landscaping and decking, from the first site visit through to the last board. He is the founder, and the installer you will meet on site. He still leads each project himself, so you never go from a salesperson to a crew you have not met.

He started the company to do the work properly: a square frame, the right fixings, and a tidy site. He lives and works near Newquay, on the north coast of Cornwall, and can reach most of the county easily.

We install composite and timber decks, steps, and glass or cable balustrades. We also repair tired or unsafe decks when a repair is the honest choice. If a full rebuild would serve you better, he will say so.
