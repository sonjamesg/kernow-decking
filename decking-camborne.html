What is the most cost-effective decking for a Camborne garden?

Treated softwood has the lowest upfront cost. Composite costs more initially but needs far less maintenance. We will price both so you can compare.

Do you cover Pool, Troon and Gwithian?

Yes, we cover Camborne and nearby areas including Pool, Troon, Beacon and Gwithian.

Can you replace the boards but keep my existing frame?

If the frame is sound and spaced correctly for the new boards, yes. We will inspect it first.
