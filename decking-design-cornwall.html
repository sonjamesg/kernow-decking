Good decking starts with good design. We visit your home, look at levels, aspect, shelter and views, and design a garden deck that makes the most of your outdoor space, whether that is a sun trap, an evening terrace or a sea-view platform.

Designed for Cornish gardens

Many Cornish gardens slope, sit on rock or face the prevailing south-westerly wind. We design around those challenges with multi-level decks, steps, raised platforms and screening that turn awkward spaces into usable ones.

We think about where the sun falls through the day, where you want shelter, and how the deck links to your house and garden.

What our design includes

A site visit and measured survey, a clear layout showing levels, steps and balustrades, material and colour options for composite or timber boards, and a fixed, itemised quote.

We can include lighting, built-in seating, planters, pergolas and hot tub bases.

Planning and permissions

Decks more than 30cm above ground level, decks at listed buildings and some decks in conservation areas may need permission from Cornwall Council. We will flag anything that needs checking before work begins.

FAQ

Decking design in Cornwall: FAQs

Can't see your question? Call 07700 900461 or send us a message.

Do you charge for a design visit?

Our site survey and quote are free and come with no obligation.

Can you design a deck for a steep garden?

Yes. Sloping gardens are common in Cornwall and are often where a deck adds the most usable space. We design raised and multi-level decks with safe steps and balustrades.

Can I just buy the design and materials?

Yes. We can provide a design and supply the materials if you, or your own builder, plan to install it.

Do I need planning permission for decking in Cornwall?

Most ground-level garden decks do not, but decks more than 30cm above ground, at listed buildings or in some conservation areas may. We will advise and help you check with Cornwall Council.
