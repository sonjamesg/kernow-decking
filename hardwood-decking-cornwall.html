Nothing quite matches the look, feel and smell of a real timber deck. We design and build hardwood and softwood decks in Cornwall, choosing timber, fixings and finishes that suit your home and how exposed it is to the weather.

Hardwood or softwood?

Dense hardwoods are naturally durable and very hard-wearing, and they weather to an elegant silver if left untreated. They suit premium terraces, steps and high-traffic decks.

Pressure-treated softwood is a cost-effective option for garden decks and larger platforms. Properly specified and maintained, it gives many years of service. We will explain the trade-offs honestly so you can choose what suits your budget.

Timber that copes with Cornish weather

Cornwall’s mild but wet climate is hard on timber. We use stainless steel fixings to avoid rust staining near the sea, leave proper gaps between boards for drainage, and keep timber off the ground with good airflow underneath.

We can pre-finish boards with oil to bring out the grain and slow down weathering, or leave them to silver naturally.

Responsibly sourced

We aim to use timber from responsibly managed sources and can talk you through certified options when you choose your materials.

How long does a timber deck last?

It depends on the timber, the build quality and how it is looked after. A well-built hardwood deck can last for decades; treated softwood lasts well when it is kept clean, ventilated and re-oiled regularly.

Does a hardwood deck need oiling?

Not necessarily. Many hardwoods can be left to weather to a silver-grey. If you want to keep the original warm colour, an oil once or twice a year is the answer.

Can you build steps and raised timber decks?

Yes. We build steps, raised platforms and multi-level decks. Decks more than 30cm above the ground usually need planning permission, and raised decks need properly designed guarding, which we will advise on.

Are timber decks slippery?

Timber can become slippery when algae builds up, especially in shade. Grooved boards, anti-slip inserts and regular cleaning all help, and we can recommend the best option for your deck.
