Glass & steel balustrades in Cornwall

Glass, stainless steel and timber balustrades for decks and terraces in Cornwall. Keep the sea view and stay safe with balustrading designed for coastal conditions.

Get a free decking quoteCall 07700 900461

In Cornwall the view is often the whole point of a deck. Our glass, stainless steel and wire balustrades keep raised decks, steps and terraces safe without blocking sea, harbour or countryside views.

Balustrade options

Frameless glass gives an uninterrupted view and a sleek, modern finish. Posted glass, with stainless steel or aluminium posts, is a strong, cost-effective alternative.

Stainless steel wire or rod balustrades suit a nautical look and let wind pass through, while timber balustrades with steel or glass infills tie in with a natural deck.

Built for wind and salt

On exposed coastal sites balustrades have to cope with strong wind loads and salt-laden air. We use marine-grade stainless steel where it is needed and choose glass and fixings to suit the location and the loads involved.

Glass on exposed decks does need regular cleaning to remove salt and spray. We will explain how to look after it.

Safety first

Raised decks, steps and changes in level need guarding that is the right height and strength. We design balustrades with safety and the relevant guidance in mind, and we can supply balustrade kits for trade customers.

Glass & steel balustrades in Cornwall: FAQs

Can't see your question? Call 07700 900461 or send us a message.

Is glass balustrading safe on a windy coastal deck?

Yes, when it is properly specified. The type and thickness of glass, the fixing method and the posts are chosen to suit the expected loads on your site.

How do I keep glass balustrades clean near the sea?

Salt and spray will mark glass, so a regular rinse with fresh water and a non-abrasive glass cleaner keeps it clear. Some coatings make the glass easier to clean.

Can you add balustrades to my existing deck?

Often, yes, as long as the deck structure is strong enough to take the balustrade posts. We will inspect it first and strengthen the frame if needed.

Do you supply balustrade kits?

Yes, we can supply balustrade components for DIY and trade projects. Send us your measurements for a price.
